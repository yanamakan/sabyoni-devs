Introduction to HealthSync

HealthSync is a desktop and mobile wellness application designed to support users after receiving a medical diagnosis or entering a new phase of health management. It helps users track symptoms, manage medications, prepare for follow-up appointments, and explore both conventional and alternative care options.

Built with C#, Python, and SQLite, HealthSync is designed to be intuitive, secure, and emotionally supportive.

The Problem

After receiving a diagnosis, many patients feel overwhelmed. They’re handed prescriptions, pamphlets, and vague instructions — but no structured way to manage their daily health.

They forget medications, miss subtle symptoms, arrive unprepared for follow-ups, and feel disconnected from their own care journey. Most apps are either too clinical, too fragmented, or locked behind subscriptions.

User Story & Business Analysis

User Story: Patients managing a new diagnosis want a simple way to track their symptoms, medications, and appointments so that they can stay in control of their health journey and feel supported every day.

Business Analysis: HealthSync bridges the gap between medical appointments and daily self-care by integrating symptom tracking, medication management, and educational resources — all in one app. This promotes empowerment, structure, and emotional clarity in the recovery process.
