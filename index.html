The Problem

After receiving a diagnosis, many patients feel overwhelmed. They're handed prescriptions, pamphlets, and vague instructions — but no structured way to manage their daily health.

Patients forget medications, miss subtle symptoms, arrive unprepared for follow-ups, and feel disconnected from their own care journey. Most apps are either too clinical, too fragmented, or locked behind subscriptions.

Solution Overview

HealthSync is a desktop-based health management application designed to bridge the gap between diagnosis and daily health management. It provides patients with tools to track their symptoms, manage medications, and access verified health information, empowering them to live healthier, more organized lives.
